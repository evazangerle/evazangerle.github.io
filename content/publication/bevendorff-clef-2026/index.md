abstract: 'For almost two decades now, the PAN lab has been striving to advance the
  state of the art in text forensics and stylometry through objective evaluation of
  new and established methods on curated benchmark datasets. In 2026, we organized
  five shared tasks: (1) Generative AI detection, particularly in mixed and obfuscated
  authorship scenarios, (2) Text Watermarking, a new task aiming to evaluate inconspicuousness
  and robustness of text watermarking techniques, (3) Multi-author Writing Style Analysis,
  a continued task aiming at identifying authorship position changes, (4) Generative
  Plagiarism Detection, focusing this year on generated text source retrieval, and
  (5) Reasoning Trajectory Detection, another new task aiming at source identification
  and safety assessment of generative reasoning chains. PAN 2026 concluded successfully
  with more than 200 software submissions and 52 notebook papers.'
